Module specifications

Every module stated as purpose, inputs, outputs, dependencies, and the edge cases that will actually break it in production. The edge-case row is the useful one — it is where the estimate doubles.

2.A  Restaurant OS — Plane A

A-01
Point of Sale
Plane A
Purpose
Capture every order regardless of channel into one state machine, take payment, and keep working when the network does not.
Inputs
Staff taps, barcode/QR scans, card reader events, cash drawer events, marketplace order injections, phone orders, kiosk sessions, published menu version.
Outputs
order + order_events, payment intent/capture, kitchen ticket, printed/emailed receipt, journal entries, order.lifecycle.v1.
Depends on
svc-menu (version pin), svc-payment, svc-kitchen, svc-ledger, local gateway, device registry.
Edge cases
Split tender across 4 cards and cash; partial refund after a tip adjust; item 86'd after the guest paid; two terminals editing one open ticket; drawer opened without a sale; shift change mid-order; tax rate change at midnight during a late-night service; comp/void by a manager who then clocks out; duplicate submit from a double-tap on a laggy screen; order placed against a menu version that was superseded 200ms earlier.
A-02
Menu management & sync
Plane A
Purpose
Hold one menu of record, publish immutable versions, and propagate them everywhere within 20 seconds.
Inputs
Console edits, CSV/POS import, AI optimizer proposals, availability toggles from KDS, dayparting rules, channel price overrides.
Outputs
Versioned snapshot + content hash in S3, CDN-cached menu JSON, menu.published.v1, per-channel projections (POS, storefront, marketplace, print).
Depends on
S3/CDN, search reindex, POS sync adapters.
Edge cases
Modifier group referenced by a deleted item; circular modifier nesting; a price of $0 that is legitimate (free side) vs. a typo; dayparting across DST transitions; a location in a different tax jurisdiction than its org; 3,000-item menus from a large franchise; unicode and emoji in item names breaking a 40-column thermal printer; the same physical dish sold at three prices on three channels.
A-03
Kitchen routing & KDS
Plane A
Purpose
Turn an order into station-level work, sequence it so items finish together, and show the line what to make next.
Inputs
Confirmed orders, station map, per-item prep times, current queue depth, staffing level, promised time, courier ETA.
Outputs
Station tickets, bump/recall state, all-day counts, expo view, kitchen.ticket.v1, actual prep-time telemetry feeding the model.
Depends on
svc-order, svc-device, dispatch ETA, local gateway (must run on LAN alone).
Edge cases
Item spans two stations (burger + fryer) and must be coordinated; a station goes offline mid-ticket; recall after bump when the guest changes their mind; rush of 40 orders in 90 seconds; large catering order that must be sequenced around walk-ins; delivery courier arrives early and the food is not ready; delivery courier never arrives and food sits; "fire on arrival" for curbside; a ticket printed but the screen never bumped.
A-04
Offline gateway
Plane A
Purpose
Guarantee a full service window with no internet, then reconcile without losing or duplicating a cent.
Inputs
Local writes from terminals/KDS, cloud inbound stream, device heartbeats, HLC timestamps.
Outputs
Durable local commits, signed outbox batches, sync status banner, reconciliation report with any variances itemized.
Depends on
Embedded Postgres, MQTT LAN bus, device certs, cloud sync endpoint.
Edge cases
Gateway hardware dies with an unsynced outbox (mitigation: terminals also retain a shadow outbox); clock jumps backward; two gateways paired to one location after a hardware swap; outbox exceeds disk; reconnect during a partial batch; a cloud-side schema migration deployed while a gateway was offline for six days; power loss mid-write; someone unplugs it to charge a phone.
A-05
Payments & settlement
Plane A
Purpose
Authorize, capture, refund, and pay out — with a ledger that reconciles to the penny against the processor and the bank.
Inputs
Payment intents, EMV/NFC reader results, saved tokens, tips, refund requests, processor webhooks, payout schedule.
Outputs
Captures, refunds, balanced journal entries, daily payouts, fee statements, 1099-K data, dispute packets.
Depends on
Processor (Stripe Connect / Adyen for Platforms), svc-ledger, bank rails.
Edge cases
Auth succeeds, capture fails; tip added after auth exceeds the 20% overage allowance; chargeback on an order already paid out; negative balance after refunds exceed daily volume; processor webhook arrives before the API response; duplicate webhook 48h later; cash-only day; refund of a partially loyalty-paid order (which pot gives back what?); a location closing its bank account mid-payout cycle.
A-06
Accounting automation
Plane A
Purpose
Close the day automatically and hand a bookkeeper something they do not have to re-key.
Inputs
Journal entries, payouts, fees, tips, comps/voids, cash counts, labor hours, tax categories.
Outputs
Daily close packet, P&L summary, sales tax accrual by jurisdiction, tip pool allocation, QuickBooks/Xero sync, variance report.
Depends on
svc-ledger, tax rate service (Avalara or internal table), payroll integration.
Edge cases
Cash drawer short by $7.14; a manager voids a settled order the next morning; multi-jurisdiction delivery where tax follows the destination; tax-exempt catering customers; tips declared vs. tips paid via card; a business day that spans 4am close; a location changing its fiscal calendar; a state changing prepared-food tax mid-quarter.
A-07
Hardware & device management
Plane A
Purpose
Pair, monitor, update, and remotely diagnose every terminal, printer, KDS, reader, and gateway without a truck roll.
Inputs
Pairing codes, heartbeats, printer status (paper, cover, jam), firmware manifests, network diagnostics.
Outputs
Device registry, health dashboard, staged OTA rollouts, alerts, remote reprint/test-print, RMA workflow.
Depends on
MQTT broker, cert authority, OTA channel, gateway.
Edge cases
Printer out of paper at 7pm Friday; a device on a guest Wi-Fi VLAN that blocks mDNS; a firmware update bricking a terminal mid-service (mitigation: staged rings + automatic rollback + never update during business hours); stolen terminal; two locations sharing one physical printer; ESC/POS dialect differences across printer vendors; a KDS screen that sleeps and misses tickets.

2.B  AI Growth Engine — Plane B

B-01
AI website builder
Plane B
Purpose
Produce a fast, SEO-correct, ordering-enabled site from a restaurant's existing footprint in under ten minutes, with no design decisions required from the operator.
Inputs
Business name + address, Google Business Profile, existing site scrape, menu snapshot, uploaded/generated photography, brand colors extracted from a logo, cuisine class.
Outputs
Deployed site on a custom domain, structured page model (not raw HTML), Restaurant/Menu/Offer schema.org JSON-LD, sitemap, OG images, Core Web Vitals budget met, ordering deep links.
Depends on
LLM (copy + IA), image pipeline, CDN/edge renderer, svc-menu, domain/DNS automation, SSL issuance.
Edge cases
No existing web presence at all; a scraped site with wrong hours; a restaurant that shares a name with a chain; domain locked at a registrar the owner cannot access; DNS propagation during a launch promo; allergen claims hallucinated into item copy (hard-blocked by the claims filter); a menu with 3,000 items needing pagination and internal linking; multi-location under one brand with per-location hours and menus.
B-02
CRM & identity resolution
Plane B
Purpose
Collapse fragmented order history across POS, web, phone, and marketplace into one profile per human, per restaurant.
Inputs
Order records, phone/email at checkout, card fingerprints, loyalty scans, device IDs, marketplace account links, imported lists.
Outputs
Unified profiles, RFM segments, churn probability, predicted LTV, favorite items, suppression lists, audience exports.
Depends on
svc-order, svc-identity, ClickHouse, ML scoring jobs.
Edge cases
Two people sharing a phone number (household); one person with three emails; a typo'd email that matches nobody; a shared corporate card producing a false merge; a merge that must be undone after a complaint; a guest exercising deletion while an order is in flight; office managers ordering for 30 people (one profile, wildly non-representative behavior); a restaurant importing a purchased list with no consent record (reject the import).
B-03
Campaign automation
Plane B
Purpose
Run lifecycle marketing that a busy operator never has to think about, and prove incremental revenue rather than attributed revenue.
Inputs
Segments, triggers (first order, lapsed 30d, birthday, cart abandon, post-delivery), offer templates, consent + preferences, send-time model output.
Outputs
SMS/email/push sends, journey state, holdout-based incrementality reports, revenue per send, opt-out rate, deliverability health.
Depends on
svc-crm, svc-notify, Twilio/SES, offer engine, uplift model.
Edge cases
Guest is in five journeys at once (frequency cap arbitration); a 40%-off blast sent to people who would have ordered anyway (this is why holdouts are mandatory); quiet hours across timezones for a traveling guest; a carrier filtering the short code; a restaurant closed on the day a "come back today" offer lands; an offer redeemable at a location that has since shut; STOP received mid-journey; a birthday on Feb 29.
B-04
Loyalty & tiers
Plane B
Purpose
Run a cross-brand points wallet that is attractive to guests, fair to restaurants, and correctly accounted for as a liability.
Inputs
Settled orders, earn rules, tier thresholds, redemption requests, expiration policy, promotional multipliers.
Outputs
Point ledger entries, balance projection, tier state, redemption authorizations, liability balance, breakage recognition, inter-org settlement.
Depends on
svc-ledger, svc-order, svc-identity.
Edge cases
Points earned at restaurant A, redeemed at restaurant B — who funds the discount? (Answer: a platform-operated clearing account with monthly net settlement, funded from the redeeming org at a fixed cents-per-point and credited to the earning org, with the spread as platform revenue.) Also: refund of an order that earned points already spent; tier downgrade mid-year; a restaurant leaving the platform with outstanding liability; points expiring under state escheatment law; double-earn from a duplicate sync; a guest gaming a referral loop.
B-05
Rewards & gamification
Plane B
Purpose
Raise order frequency through streaks, challenges, and badges without degrading into a coupon race.
Inputs
Order cadence, category coverage, local-explorer signals, seasonal events, referral graph.
Outputs
Achievement grants, streak state, challenge progress, bonus point grants, shareable cards.
Depends on
svc-loyalty, svc-notify, svc-marketplace.
Edge cases
Streak broken by the restaurant being closed (grace day); a challenge that is only completable by overspending (cap challenge cost as % of guest AOV); collusive referral rings; a badge referencing a delisted restaurant; users gaming with $0 orders; achievements that create real liability without a budget cap.
B-06
Upsell engine
Plane B
Purpose
Raise AOV at the point of decision with suggestions that are relevant, in stock, fast to prep, and high margin — in that priority order.
Inputs
Cart contents, person history, item affinities (co-purchase lift), current 86 list, station load, item margin and prep time.
Outputs
Ranked add-on slate per cart stage, bundle proposals, attach-rate telemetry, revenue lift vs. holdout.
Depends on
svc-recs, svc-menu availability, svc-kitchen load.
Edge cases
Suggesting an item that is 86'd (never — hard filter); suggesting a drink to a cart that already has four; allergen conflict with a stated preference; suggesting a 25-minute item onto a 6-minute order and blowing the promised time; upsell fatigue suppressing conversion; a bundle whose discount inverts margin.
B-07
AI menu optimizer
Plane B
Purpose
Recommend price, placement, naming, and description changes that raise contribution margin, with an estimated effect and a confidence interval attached to every proposal.
Inputs
Item-level sales history, cost, prep time, modifier attach rates, competitor price scrapes, elasticity estimates, seasonality, weather.
Outputs
Ranked change proposals with projected Δmargin and CI, menu-engineering quadrant (star / plow-horse / puzzle / dog), rewritten item copy, A/B test plans.
Depends on
svc-menu, svc-analytics, LLM (copy), elasticity model.
Edge cases
Thin data on a new item (fall back to category priors and say so); price increases that breach a psychological threshold ($9.99→$10.49); an item whose demand is driven entirely by a lapsed promotion; regulated pricing (alcohol, combo meals in some jurisdictions); an operator who accepts 30 proposals at once and destabilizes the whole menu (rate-limit to 5 changes per 14 days); cost data that is stale or absent.
B-08
AI labor scheduling
Plane B
Purpose
Publish a schedule that matches forecast demand at target labor percentage while respecting availability and law.
Inputs
Demand forecast by 15-min bucket, staff availability, skills/stations, wage rates, labor rules, historical throughput per staff-hour.
Outputs
Draft schedule, projected labor %, overtime warnings, shift-swap marketplace, actual-vs-forecast variance.
Depends on
Forecast model, svc-tenant (staff), payroll integration.
Edge cases
Predictive-scheduling ordinances (Oregon, NYC, SF, Chicago, Philadelphia) requiring 14-day advance notice and premium pay for changes — jurisdiction rules must be a data table, not code; minors with hour restrictions; a no-show 20 minutes before open; a forecast blown by a street festival or a snowstorm; a schedule that is optimal but unfair (same person always closing).

2.C  Marketplace — Plane C

C-01
Consumer app & discovery
Plane C
Purpose
Give guests a reason to open Mobile Dinners instead of DoorDash: better prices, one wallet across every local restaurant, and honest timing.
Inputs
Location, time, person history and embedding, query text, filters, live availability and capacity.
Outputs
Ranked feed, search results, storefronts, cart, checkout, order tracking, wallet, achievements.
Depends on
svc-search, svc-recs, svc-marketplace, svc-tracking, svc-identity.
Edge cases
Empty market at launch (cold start — show pickup + a curated set, never an empty grid); guest outside every service radius; location permission denied; a restaurant that goes offline between feed render and checkout; a stale cart against a republished menu; address ambiguity in apartment complexes; a guest ordering from a different city than their saved address.
C-02
Listings & storefronts
Plane C
Purpose
Present each restaurant accurately — real menu, real availability, real wait — and convert.
Inputs
Menu snapshot, 86 overlay, hours + exceptions, photos, reviews, live kitchen load, fee structure.
Outputs
Storefront page, item detail with modifiers, quoted prep window, fee breakdown, "closes in 20 min" states.
Depends on
svc-menu, svc-kitchen, svc-dispatch.
Edge cases
Restaurant open but kitchen overloaded (show a longer honest wait, not "unavailable"); scheduled orders past closing; a holiday exception nobody entered; a photo showing an item that is 86'd; a menu that differs by fulfillment type; a restaurant that pauses mid-cart.
C-03
Cross-restaurant loyalty wallet
Plane C
Purpose
One balance, every participating restaurant. The consumer-facing reason the network is worth more than the sum of its restaurants.
Inputs
Settled orders across all orgs, tier state, redemption intents.
Outputs
Wallet balance and history, redeemable offers by location, tier benefits, inter-org settlement records.
Depends on
svc-loyalty, svc-ledger, svc-identity.
Edge cases
An org opting out of accepting foreign points (allowed — must be visible before checkout, not after); redemption at a location whose owner disputes the clearing rate; concurrent redemption from two devices (serializable isolation, reserve-then-commit); points from a refunded order already spent; a guest in a market with only one participating restaurant (wallet feels pointless — gate the pitch on market density).
C-04
Real-time order tracking
Plane C
Purpose
Show the guest true state — and specifically not a fake progress bar that advances on a timer.
Inputs
Order events, kitchen ticket state, courier assignment and location, traffic.
Outputs
Live status, map with courier position, revised ETA with reason, delivery photo, rating prompt.
Depends on
svc-tracking, svc-dispatch, svc-kitchen, WebSocket infra.
Edge cases
Courier phone loses signal in a parking garage (show last-known + timestamp, never a frozen dot pretending to be live); ETA slipping repeatedly (notify once with a reason, then stop); guest closes the app (push handles the rest); driver takes a personal detour (privacy vs. transparency — coarse position only); delivered-but-not-received disputes; a batched delivery where the guest is second.
C-05
Dispatch & driver network
Plane C
Purpose
Get food from kitchen to door at a cost the model supports, using the cheapest adequate supply source available.
Inputs
Ready-time prediction, courier positions and states, order geography, batching constraints, pay model, third-party quotes.
Outputs
Assignments and offers, batches, routes, ETAs, courier payouts, cost-per-delivery telemetry.
Depends on
svc-kitchen, courier APIs, Redis geo, routing engine (OSRM/Valhalla or Mapbox).
Edge cases
No courier accepts (escalate pay in steps, then fall back to third party, then offer the guest pickup with a credit); courier accepts then abandons; restaurant is 25 minutes late and the courier is waiting (compensate wait time or lose supply); rain spikes demand and collapses supply simultaneously; multi-unit apartment with no access code; a batched order where restaurant #2 is late; a courier marking delivered from three blocks away (geofence + photo required).
C-06
Personalized recommendations
Plane C
Purpose
Make the second and third order happen without a discount.
Inputs
Behavioral events, order history, embeddings, context (time, weather, day, location), exploration policy.
Outputs
Feed slates, "order again" rails, item-level suggestions, notification-worthy moments.
Depends on
Feature store, model serving, svc-search.
Edge cases
Brand-new user with zero history; a user whose only order was a one-off catering job; over-personalization producing a feed of the same three restaurants (diversity constraint); recommending a place that is closed or out of range; dietary constraints stated once and then violated (hard filter, not a ranking feature); a model outage (fall back to popularity-by-hex, never an error state).
C-07
Ratings, reviews & trust
Plane C
Purpose
Give guests signal and operators feedback, while separating food quality from delivery performance — a distinction every aggregator gets wrong and every restaurant resents.
Inputs
Post-order prompts, order + delivery telemetry, photos, operator responses.
Outputs
Two independent scores (food, delivery), verified-order badges, sentiment themes, operator response workflow, fraud flags.
Depends on
svc-order, svc-dispatch, moderation model.
Edge cases
A 1-star for a late delivery on a restaurant that had the food ready in 6 minutes (never counted against the restaurant); review bombing by a competitor; a review of an order that was never placed; extortion ("refund me or I post"); a legitimate complaint about an allergen incident that requires escalation, not moderation; an operator response revealing guest PII.
