User flows

Each flow is specified with its happy path, its measured target, and the branch that actually happens — the failure branch is where the product is won or lost.

5.1  Restaurant onboarding

The single most important flow in the company. Every hour of onboarding friction is a percentage point of activation. Target: a live storefront in 20 minutes, a live POS in one scheduled session.

Onboarding rule

Steps 1–9 must never depend on steps 10–14. A restaurant that signs up on Monday should be taking online orders on its own domain on Monday, before hardware ships and before the marketplace review completes. Coupling the fast path to the slow path is the most common way a vertical SaaS company converts a signup into a refund.

5.7  POS sync flow

Covered structurally in §1.7. The operational flow that matters is the cutover, because that is the moment a restaurant decides whether it trusts the platform.

Fig. 05.4 — POS cutover runbookMD-FLOW-504
  T−7d   Menu parity audit. Automated diff old POS vs. MD. Every mismatch
         resolved and signed off by the operator. Zero open diffs is the gate.
  T−3d   Hardware on-site, on the network, test tickets printed at every station.
  T−1d   Staff walkthrough (6 min/role). Cheat-sheet card printed and taped
         to each terminal. Manager PIN set. Rollback rehearsed once.
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  T=0    Tuesday 9:00am. Never Friday. Never before a holiday.
         Old POS stays powered and reachable for 7 days.
  T+0h   Open on MD. MD staff present (on-site for the first 20 accounts,
         on video after). Both systems can take an order.
  T+2h   Go/no-go checkpoint. Abort criteria, decided in advance:
           · >3 tickets misrouted, OR
           · any payment failure not attributable to the card, OR
           · average order entry time > 1.4× the old POS
         Any one triggers rollback. Rollback is < 10 minutes and is
         not treated as a failure — it is treated as the system working.
  T+1d   Daily close reconciled against the old POS's last full day.
  T+7d   Old POS decommissioned. Historical data exported and imported
         into MD so the CRM starts with real history, not an empty list.
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Measured: time-to-first-order, tickets-per-hour vs. baseline, void rate,
            staff-reported friction (single question, day 1 / day 7 / day 30)
