Scaling posture

The system is read-heavy on Plane C and write-heavy on Plane A, and those loads peak at the same moment: 6–8pm local. Scale reads with CDN + Redis + ClickHouse replicas; scale writes by partitioning Postgres on business_date and sharding by org_id only when a single primary genuinely saturates (realistically past ~4,000 locations). Do not shard early — cross-shard order/ledger consistency is a category of bug you should buy your way out of with bigger hardware for as long as possible.

MD-04AI SystemsSection 04 of 108 subsystems

AI system design

Eight subsystems, one orchestrator, one rule: every AI action is logged as a decision with its inputs, its proposal, the human verdict, and the measured outcome. Without that loop the models never improve and no claim about them is falsifiable.

4.1  Model portfolio and routing

Task class determines model class. The orchestrator routes on capability, latency budget, and cost ceiling — not on a hardcoded model name scattered through the codebase. A prompt registry versions every template; a change to a prompt is a deploy with an eval gate, exactly like a code change.

Hard rule — no generated food

Diffusion may relight, crop, clean a background, or upscale a real photograph of a real dish. It may not synthesize an image of a dish the kitchen has never plated. Guests order from photos; a synthetic photo is a consumer-protection problem and a refund generator. Every processed image carries a provenance record and the original is retained.

4.3  Menu optimization engine

The optimizer is not "an LLM that suggests prices." It is a constrained optimization over an estimated demand model, with an LLM used only for the parts that are genuinely language: naming and description. Keeping those two concerns separate is what makes the output defensible to a skeptical operator.

4.4  Marketing automation and uplift

The standard failure of restaurant marketing tools is reporting attributed revenue: send 10,000 texts, count everyone who ordered in 72 hours, declare victory. Most of those people were going to order anyway. Mobile Dinners reports incremental revenue against a mandatory holdout, which will produce smaller and truer numbers — and is the only honest basis for the ROI claim in the sales deck.

Conflict of interest, stated plainly

Cross-brand discovery moves an order from restaurant A to restaurant B. That is good for the network and bad for restaurant A on that night. The policy: cross-brand promotion happens only in marketplace surfaces the platform owns, never inside a restaurant's own storefront, app, or email list; it never targets a guest who ordered from A in the last 14 days; and the resulting order still pays A nothing and B nothing in commission. Restaurants can see this policy in writing. Getting caught quietly poaching from your own customers is the fastest way to lose a restaurant network.

Guardrails that ship with V1

  • Claims filter. A deterministic classifier plus a rules list blocks generated text containing allergen assertions ("gluten-free", "nut-free"), health claims, origin claims ("locally sourced"), and superlatives that create liability. Blocked spans are shown to the operator with an explanation, not silently removed.
  • Action bounds. Price changes capped per cycle and per window; discounts cannot push an item below a margin floor; campaign sends capped per guest per week and per org per day; no autonomous action may create financial exposure above a per-tenant ceiling.
  • Autonomy ladder. Every AI capability starts at suggest. It advances to auto-with-undo only after 30 accepted suggestions at ≥80% acceptance for that tenant, and to autonomous only for reversible, bounded actions. The ladder is per-tenant and per-capability, and it can be walked back automatically if acceptance drops.
  • Decision log. Every proposal writes ai.decision.v1 with model version, prompt version, feature snapshot, proposal, human verdict, and the measured 21-day outcome. This is the eval set, the audit trail, and the dispute record simultaneously.
  • Cost governor. Per-tenant and per-task token budgets with hard stops; the router degrades to a cheaper model before it degrades availability, and never silently — the operator sees which tier produced a result.
  • Data boundary. Tenant data is never used to train a model whose outputs are served to a different tenant, except as aggregated statistics with a minimum cohort size of 25. This is a contractual commitment, and it is enforced in the feature pipeline, not in a policy document.
